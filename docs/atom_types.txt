Pedagogical Atom Types
======================

Each atom has a "type" field that classifies the cognitive skill being developed.
Valid values and their meanings:

  definition   State a precise definition of a key term.
               e.g. "Define price elasticity of demand."

  concept      Explain a concept, mechanism, or relationship in your own words.
               Maps broadly to AQA mark scheme AO1.
               e.g. "Explain the difference between a movement along and a shift
               of the demand curve."

  diagram      Draw, label, and/or interpret a specific diagram.
               Treated as a distinct skill because students can understand a
               concept verbally while still being unable to draw it correctly.
               e.g. "Draw a correctly labelled production possibility frontier."

  calculation  Apply a formula or method to compute a numerical result.
               e.g. "Calculate PED given price and quantity data."

  application  Use a concept or model to analyse an unfamiliar real-world context.
               Maps broadly to AQA mark scheme AO2.
               e.g. "Explain how a rise in interest rates affects investment."

  evaluation   Assess strengths, limitations, or trade-offs of a theory, policy,
               or argument. Requires forming a judgement, not just understanding.
               Maps broadly to AQA mark scheme AO3.
               e.g. "Evaluate the effectiveness of a minimum wage in reducing
               poverty."

  recall       Memorise a specific fact, statistic, or institutional detail where
               no deeper explanation is required.
               e.g. "The MPC's inflation target is 2%."
               Distinct from 'definition': recall covers loose facts; definition
               requires precision and implies understanding.


Notes on overlap
----------------
- "definition" vs "recall": definitions require precision and often imply
  understanding; recall covers facts that don't need to be explained.
- "concept" vs "application": concept = abstract understanding of the idea;
  application = deploying it in a new context. These map to AO1 and AO2.
- "diagram" is separate because it is a distinct skill in economics exams.
- "evaluation" is essential for A-level and genuinely differs from understanding —
  it requires forming and defending a judgement.
